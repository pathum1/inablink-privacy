Privacy Policy for the "Page Poster" app · Last updated 22 November 2026

Who we are

"Page Poster" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page In a Blink to publish videos and posts to that Page. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app uses

  • It uses a Page access token, granted by the Page owner, to publish content to the In a Blink Page and to read that Page's own post statistics.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data about people who view, like, comment on or follow the Page.
  • It does not use cookies, tracking or advertising identifiers.

How data is stored

The only stored item is the Page owner's own access token, kept on the owner's private computer and never shared. A local log records which posts the app published (post ID, time, caption).

Data deletion

The app holds no data about Facebook users other than the Page owner. If you believe the app holds data about you, or you want something removed, send a message to the In a Blink Facebook Page and it will be deleted within 30 days. The Page owner can remove all of the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message the In a Blink Facebook Page.
